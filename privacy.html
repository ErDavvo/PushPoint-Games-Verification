Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PushPoint Games Verification collects, uses, and protects information when users connect their Roblox account with their Discord account.

Information We Collect

When you use our verification service, we may collect and store the following information:

  • Discord User ID
  • Roblox User ID
  • Roblox Username
  • Date and time of verification

We do not collect or store your Roblox password, Discord password, or any private authentication credentials.

How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used only for:

  • Verifying ownership of a Roblox account
  • Linking your Roblox account to your Discord account
  • Providing verification features inside the Discord server
  • Preventing fraudulent or duplicate account links

Data Storage

Your information is stored securely in our database and is only accessible by authorized administrators responsible for maintaining the verification service.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Information may only be disclosed if required by law.

Data Removal

You may request the removal of your linked Roblox account information by contacting the server staff or using an available unlink feature.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

Changes To This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the service means you accept any changes.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the PushPoint Games staff team.
