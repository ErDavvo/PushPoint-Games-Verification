Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

When you use the Roblox Verification Service, we may store:

  • Discord User ID
  • Roblox User ID

How We Use Your Information

Information is used only to:

  • Verify ownership of your Roblox account
  • Link Roblox and Discord accounts
  • Provide verification features

Security

We do not collect passwords or authentication credentials. We take reasonable measures to protect stored information.

Data Removal

Users may request removal of their linked account information by contacting server staff.

Contact
